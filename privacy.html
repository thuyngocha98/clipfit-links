1. Introduction

ClipFit: Video Compressor ("ClipFit", "the app", "we", "us") is an offline video compression app built by an independent developer. ClipFit is designed around privacy: it does its work on your device and has no servers that receive your content.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, why, and what happens to it. By using ClipFit, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Zero data collection and transmission

100% of video processing happens on your device. ClipFit does not:

  • collect, store on any server, upload, or transmit your videos, photos, audio, or their metadata;
  • collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or device identifiers;
  • include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

ClipFit's own code never connects to the internet, and we run no servers. The app's install page lists an internet permission only because it is required by Google's Play Billing library, which ClipFit uses for the optional Pro purchase (see In-app purchases). Links such as this policy open in your own web browser.

3. What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information locally, in its private app storage on your phone:

  • Compressed videos you create. They are saved to your device's video library, in the Movies/ClipFit folder. They are yours; ClipFit never sends them anywhere.
  • Temporary working files created during compression. They are removed once the compressed copy has been saved to your library. Anything left behind by an interrupted job stays in the app's private storage, which only ClipFit can access.
  • Your Pro status (whether you own ClipFit Pro), so the app works offline.

This information never leaves your device and is not included in cloud backups by the app. Uninstalling ClipFit removes its private storage. Videos you saved to your library stay there until you delete them.

Deleting originals. If you choose "Replace original", ClipFit first saves and verifies the compressed copy, then asks Android to delete the original. Android shows its own confirmation dialog, and nothing is deleted unless you tap Allow.

4. Device permissions explained

ClipFit asks only for the permissions it needs. You can review or revoke them at any time in Android Settings → Apps → ClipFit → Permissions.

Photos and videos / Storage

READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13+), READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below), WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only)

Lets you pick the videos you want to compress, lets the app find large videos that could be shrunk, and lets the app save the compressed copy to your video library. Videos are read on your device and are never uploaded.

Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+), with a foreground service for media processing

Used only to show compression progress, and the result, while the app runs in the background, including in the notification shade and on the lock screen. If you decline, compression still works; you just won't see progress notifications. We never send marketing notifications.

Location and EXIF metadata

ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: not requested

ClipFit does not ask for location access, and it never reads, uses, or tracks your location. To keep your gallery in order, the app copies the original recording date to the compressed copy using the standard Android media-library date field, which does not require location access. If a future version ever needs ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION, it will be used only to keep a video's own embedded metadata intact on your device. We will update this policy before that change ships.

5. In-app purchases

ClipFit offers an optional one-time "ClipFit Pro – Lifetime" purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play In-App Billing. We never see, receive, or store your card number, billing address, or other payment details. Google Play only tells the app whether your Google account owns ClipFit Pro, so the app can unlock Pro features and let you restore your purchase.

To make purchases work, the Google Play Billing library built into ClipFit communicates with Google Play and may send Google technical information about the purchase process, such as billing diagnostics and error reports. This data goes directly to Google, is handled by Google, and is never sent to or accessible by us. It never includes your videos.

Your payment information is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. No user accounts

ClipFit does not require, and does not offer, sign-up, log-in, or a personal account. You can use the app without giving us any personal information.

7. Third-party services

The only third-party service the app relies on is Google Play, for app distribution and purchases. When you tap an external link (such as this policy), it opens in your browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

8. Children's privacy (COPPA)

ClipFit is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information (for example, by email), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you rights to access, correct, delete, or opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Because ClipFit does not collect or store personal information on any server, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete, and we do not sell or share personal information. Your on-device data is under your control: you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if the app gains new features or permissions. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for material changes, give notice in the app or on its Google Play listing. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
